## 1. Two halves: the website, and your dashboard

**The website** is what customers see at www.insulationproducts.com. **The dashboard** is a private set of pages, behind a password, where you change it. There is nothing to "publish": when you save something in the dashboard, the website shows it within about a minute.

![The homepage, with four areas marked](rick-email/img/site-home.jpg)

1. The headline, paragraph and buttons — **Page Content → Homepage — Hero**.
2. The four small cards — **Page Content → Homepage — Hero Proof Points**.
3. The big photo — **Site Images & Logo**.
4. The **Request a Quote** button — every page has one; it opens the Contact page.

![A product page, with four areas marked](rick-email/img/site-product.jpg)

1. The product's name and part number — that product's **Edit** screen.
2. The **Datasheet** button — the product's **Manage PDF** screen.
3. The photo — the product's **Photo** screen.
4. **Request a Quote** opens the Contact page with this part number already filled in.

![The footer at the bottom of every page](rick-email/img/site-footer.png)

1. Phone, fax, email, address, hours — **Business Details**. Change the phone number once there and it changes on every page.
2. The short paragraph about IPC — **Page Content → Company Claims**.
3. Quick Links — **Page Content → Navigation — Footer Quick Links**.
4. The social media icons — **Business Details → Social Links** (an empty box removes the icon).

If you ever wonder where something on the site is changed, the dashboard's **Help** page has a section called **Where each part of the site is edited** that goes through every page, top to bottom.

## 8. Your safety net: Backups and the Audit Log

Every save keeps a copy of how things were just before it. If something ever saves wrong, open **Backups & History → Backups** and restore the entry stamped with the time you saved the change you want to undo — you can do this yourself, and a restore can itself be undone.

![Backups](rick-email/img/backups.png)

1. The time of the save, and the change that restoring it would undo.
2. **Restore This Version**.

One exception: uploading a new data sheet over an existing one (or a new photo over one you uploaded earlier) replaces the old file for good, so keep a copy of the old one first.

**Backups & History → Audit Log** lists every change made in the dashboard, newest first — handy for finding when something changed before you restore it.

![The Audit Log](rick-email/img/audit-log.png)

1. Search by part number or a word like *photo*.
2. One change: when, what kind, which product, and what was done.

## 10. Please check these — facts only you can confirm

The website says these things about IPC. Most came from the old website, and nothing on my side can prove them, so please look them over. Each one is a box in the dashboard: check it says what's true, and change or empty it if not. If it's easier, send me your answers and I'll make the changes.

**Certifications**

- **ISO 9001** (Business Details → Certifications): type it the way your current certificate shows it, including the revision year — for example **ISO 9001:2015**, not just "2015" — or empty the box if you'd rather the site not claim it. Emptying it takes it off the footer, the About page fact box and the homepage band; other places that mention ISO 9001 in their own words (some Page Content cards, the About story, and the VALUE-ADDED product) stay until you edit them — Business Details lists every one of them after you save.
- **"RoHS compliant — entire line"**: only 2 of the 42 products say RoHS on their data sheets. It appears in Page Content (About — Certifications & Standards, Homepage — Hero Trust Ticker, the RoHS question in the FAQ, and the Products & Services Cards) and in Business Details → About Page. Keep it only if it's true for every product.
- **AMS specifications**: which AMS specs does IPC actually hold? They're mentioned in Page Content (Hero Trust Ticker, About — Certifications & Standards, Industries Grid, Industries Page — Detail Sections, FAQ) and Business Details → About Page.
- **Approval marks on the products** (Products → Edit): many products say things like "U/L Approved", "CSA Approved" or "FDA Approved". UL, CSA and the FDA don't issue approvals by those names (UL, for example, *lists* or *recognizes* a product). Please tell me which marks each product really holds — then we'll get the spelling of UL, CSA, MIL-SPEC, AMS and RoHS to match your certificates.

**Promises the site makes**

- 25 million+ feet in stock, a $50 minimum order, same- or next-business-day shipping, custom work in a week or less, JIT programs, PPAP and IMDS packages, certificates of conformance — are all of these still true? (The minimum order and feet in stock are in Business Details; empty the Minimum Order box if there is no minimum.)
- A few specific claims I couldn't back up from the data sheets: "Ford LP Approved Variants", "M23053/8 QPL Available", "Class C" for the 130°C vinyl-coated fiberglass sleeving (130°C is usually Class B), cable ties as a stocked item (they're not among the 42 products), "clean room environment", services shipping "in one week or less" (the Hot-Stamp brochure says one to two weeks), and the CT conduit described as "Liquid-Tight … UL & CUL Listed" on the Industries page but "Non-Metallic Electrical Conduit Tubing" on its own page.
- **Search engines** boxes (Page Content → Company Claims): these tell Google the brand and manufacturer of every product. IPC is a distributor — if IPC doesn't make a product, correct or empty them.
