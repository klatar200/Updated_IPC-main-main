# Editing Your Site Content

A short guide to updating your website yourself — no coding, no developer needed. Everything is edited from your admin dashboard, and changes appear on the live site within about a minute.

## Signing in

1. Go to **yourdomain.com/admin** in any web browser.
2. Enter your admin password.
3. You'll land on the dashboard, with a menu across the top: **Products**, **Add Product**, **Business Details**, **Page Content**, **Audit Log**, and **Help**.

Use **View Live Site** (top right) any time to open your public site in a new tab, and **Sign Out** when you're done.

## The three things you can edit

**Products** — your catalog. Add a new product, edit an existing one, or upload/replace a product's data-sheet PDF. Use the search box to find a product quickly by name or part number.

**Business Details** — your company information wherever it appears on the site: phone, fax, email, address, hours, ISO/certification text, social links, the "About" paragraphs, and your brand colors and logo. Change a color here and the whole site re-skins.

**Page Content** — the marketing sections of your pages (explained below).

## Editing Page Content

Open the **Page Content** tab. It has two kinds of sections, one after the other.

**Page Text (the cards at the top).** These are the fixed headlines and intros on each page — fill-in-the-blank fields, one card per area:

- **Homepage Hero** — the badge, the three headline lines, the sub-headline, and both buttons (their wording and where each one links).
- **Homepage headings** — the "Products & Services" and "Industries" eyebrows, titles, and the CTA ribbon text.
- **Page banners** — the small eyebrow, big title, and intro paragraph at the top of the Services, Industries, About, FAQ, Contact, and Privacy pages, plus the About page's sub-headings and the Privacy lead paragraph and effective date.

**Lists (the sections below).** These are the repeatable cards and rows:

- **Products & Services Cards**, **Trust Bar Stats**, **Hero Proof Points**, and **Hero Trust Ticker** — the homepage.
- **Industries Grid** — the "industries we serve" cards.
- **Value-Added Services** — the Services page cards.
- **About — Timeline, Team & Capabilities, Certifications** — the About page.
- **FAQ / Resources** — your questions and answers.
- **Privacy Policy Sections** — the titled blocks of your privacy policy.
- **Navigation — Company Menu & Footer Links** — the labels in your header menu and footer.

In a **Page Text** card, just click a field and type. In a **list** section you can also:

- **Add** an item with the **+ Add** button at the bottom of the section.
- **Remove** an item with the **✕** button.
- **Reorder** items with the **↑** and **↓** buttons.

Then click **Save Content** at the bottom. That's it — your changes go live within about a minute.

## A few helpful notes

**Clearing a heading resets it.** If you empty a Page Text field and save, that item simply returns to its original wording — so a page heading can never end up blank.

**Icons come from a menu.** Cards that show an icon (services, certifications, industries, stats) have an **Icon dropdown** — pick the closest match. Team cards use an emoji you can type in the "Icon" field.

**Bullet points, one per line.** In the Services section, the bullet-points box takes one item per line — press Enter for each new bullet.

**Links stay safe.** Wherever a button or link has a "Links to" dropdown — the hero buttons, header-menu items, and footer links — you can rename it freely and the dropdown makes sure it always points at a real page, so you can't accidentally create a broken link.

**Seeing your changes.** Edits appear on the live site within about 60 seconds. If you don't see them, do a hard refresh (**Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows, **Cmd+Shift+R** on Mac).

**Backups are automatic.** Every save keeps a backup of the previous version, and the **Audit Log** tab records who changed what and when — so nothing is ever lost.

## If something looks wrong

Double-check you clicked **Save Content** (or **Save** on Business Details) and give it a full minute plus a hard refresh. If a section ever looks empty or off, your original content is safe — reach out to your developer, who can restore the previous version from the automatic backups in seconds.
